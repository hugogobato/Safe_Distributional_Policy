# Phase 4 simulation preregistration

**Freeze.** This document fixes the Phase 4 design, analysis and decision rules before inspecting any Phase 4 simulation draws. The pilot uses seed 999 and is kept outside the confirmatory seed grid. Repairs needed to correct code or assumptions will be recorded before confirmatory execution. The 50 confirmatory seeds are 1000–1049. The planned 20-seed ZIC-BCF sensitivity uses 1000–1019. ## Targets, comparators and ordering

The primary certificate intersects uncapped upper-quantile noninferiority and noninferiority in the probability of positive loss. The risk tolerances are both zero, and the family-wise nominal level is .05. The baseline is action zero. Primary beta is .90. Capped CVaR is recorded for all selected policies and tested separately in the heavy-tail sweep. Losing on capped CVaR alone does not failure-test the quantile-first scope already adopted at G2. Two-sided 95% Monte Carlo intervals describe replication precision.

- LCT-LTT and B1 share the same fitted hurdle welfare rule and safety scores. B1 reports both non-oracle two-arm survival tests and OPRA CDF bands, along with their joint extensive comparator. For decision comparisons, use the larger deployment or safe welfare of the two B1 variants, respectively.
- B2 uses the same localized guard and a training-fitted mean causal forest ranking. B3 is the training-only plug-in capped-CVaR ITR, and B4 is the unguarded B2 learner. B5 uses the same hurdle rule and the signed exceedance difference at the training-fitted baseline quantile. Run B5 with and without the identical extensive guard. The integration is an indicator-risk LTT analogue of CPC, with a training-fixed deterministic policy ladder. It uses the signed contrast regardless of the localized quantile test. The B1 survival comparator is already a strong two-arm pointwise-tail method. The relative learned-reference target differs from an external fixed budget. [Theorem-level source audit](phase2_e3_prior_art_check.md)
- All learners share the training inputs, with three folds and the same count of two hyperparameter values for the two-part boosting and causal forest. Use Phase 3 values 5 and 12 and 100 trees in the native R causal forest, with B1/B3/B5 sharing the two-part fit. ZIC-BCF uses the Phase 3 bridge and maps training-only posterior summaries to held-out covariates through its projection forest. Use 500 burn-in, 1,000 posterior draws and 200 prognostic/50 moderating trees per hurdle/intensity component. Report ZIC-BCF as a secondary projection sensitivity.
- The nominal offered deployment masses are 15, 20, 25, 30, 35, 40, 45, 50, 55, 60, 70, 80, 90, and 100 percent. Training-only scores fix the thresholds and tie handling. The diagnostic appends 5 and 10 percent at the start of that same sequence. The tests stop on the first failure. Use betting for primary LCT-LTT, with empirical Bernstein, 5%-first, and quantile-only ablations. Include B2 and matched-guard B5 in the order and quantile-only diagnostics so their interpretation can distinguish the guess of the baseline quantile, ordering and the extensive constraint. DGP 4 has no extensive change. Its joint test can therefore abstain even when the quantile improves, and the quantile-only comparison is the explicit location-case diagnostic.

## Samples and known truth

Use n_cal=5,000 for core, DGPs 7–9, and 2/3 as many training rows rounded up. For DGP 10, n is the calibration size and the same training-to-calibration ratio applies. Each replication uses independent training, calibration and 100,000 evaluation covariates drawn by NumPy SeedSequence `[DGP number, seed]` and separate child streams. A design's variations reuse underlying streams for paired comparisons. Assign row IDs before treatment assignment. The full grids in this document define the expected checkpoints.

Evaluate a policy's risks and welfare using the DGP's conditional laws at the holdout covariates. Evaluate the signed conditional survival difference at the true baseline quantile and the signed probability of positive loss. Since the baseline survival at its quantile equals 1−beta for all primary designs, a positive survival contrast is equivalent to strictly worse quantile risk. Compute the baseline quantile from the model marginal CDF, with deterministic integration over the one-dimensional smooth baseline in DGP 2. Use paired conditional-score means and standard errors for evaluating the learned switch regions. A one-sided normal critical value, Bonferroni-adjusted across the bounded number of inspected method/threshold pairs in a replication, defines definitely unsafe, definitely safe, or unresolved cases. Report definite, plug-in and possible-unsafe rates for the full accepted prefix and retain component flags. These holdout intervals quantify evaluation uncertainty and are distinct from the finite-sample calibration guarantee. Use the same approach for matched mean or tail comparators. Structural no-switch/equality cases and uniform strict-harm designs permit sign adjudication from the model. Report incomplete classification separately. Welfare uses model conditional means of utility −loss. Safety-adjusted welfare is zero for unsafe final policies, with a separate lower summary setting unresolved contributions to zero. Sample quantile and capped CVaR differences from held-out potential losses accompany the primary conditional truth check as secondary descriptive results.

The oracle enumerates the same nominal fractions of prioritized covariate regions, with direction set from the true DGP. It selects the best safe welfare candidate on the holdout set and is a descriptive benchmark over its own offered family. In DGP 4 and DGP 5 the relevant oracle family may offer an all-treatment or prespecified beneficial region threshold. Never train a deployable score or order using evaluation data.

## Data-generating ladder

Conditional on a positive outcome, log-loss is Normal with the indicated mu and sigma, or Pareto with indicated minimum and exponent in DGP 8. The arms share the hurdle uniforms and residuals to improve precision in descriptive diagnostics. Randomized logging is independent of potential losses given X. In DGPs 1–6 and 8, e(1|X)=.25+.5X1. ## Gate interpretation and outputs

1. Credibility. For each known-propensity primary DGP/cell require empirical FWER at most .065, a possible-unsafe rate at most .065 for a clean pass, and inspect definite rates over .08 before proceeding. Report exact binomial intervals, Monte Carlo ambiguity and boundary diagnostics. In the strict near-null require at least 95% abstention for the prespecified nonempty ladder.
2. Localization. On mechanism-favorable DGP 5 and focal DGP 6 (s=.25, n_cal=5,000, beta=.90), require at least +20 percentage points certified deployment or +25% mean safe welfare relative to the stronger B1. Gains below 10 points with no meaningful welfare gain are evidence of practical collapse.
3. Scoring. The main plan's work-package text names DGP 4 as mean-tail conflict while its DGP ladder defines it as pure location. Apply the +15% paired mean safe-welfare threshold to mean-tail conflict DGP 5 and the MEPS-like DGP 9 stress. Keep DGP 4 as the honest favorable control for B2. For relative comparisons with zero baseline welfare, require a strictly positive gain supported by the paired 95% interval. Report raw differences and deployment alongside relative gains.
4. Indicator alternative. Require better quantile safety at meaningful deployment or at least +15% safe welfare relative to B5 at comparable safety. Evaluate the fixed-cutoff B5 with and without the matched extensive guard. A contrast caused solely by the missing extensive guard is insufficient to support the comparative quantile claim. Also tabulate B3/B4 violations, including the original plan's 2δ benchmark.
5. Stress. Identify observed grid boundaries rather than interpolating an unsupported exact crossover. Report Gamma sensitivity under the known inequality, cap sensitivity, conditional truth diagnostics, and resource scaling. Treat failures as scientific or implementation failures only with evidence from the documented checks. Retain ZIC-BCF as a candidate engine only if the projected paired welfare gain exceeds 3% and the runtime is practical.
6. Interpretation. GO or scoped PIVOT requires the relevant criteria above across the complete confirmatory suite. Full collapse against a matched comparator on all primary mechanism-favorable regimes, systematic unsafe certification or persistent zero deployment triggers KILL for the tested scope. Modest gains, instability or failure to clear the practical effect gates triggers INCREMENTAL-ONLY. Ambiguity from unavailable shards or material outcome-classification uncertainty leaves the gate pending. Core evidence alone is identified as preliminary evidence for this joint gate, with immediate debugging for a confirmed credibility violation.

Record a complete checkpoint for every DGP/configuration/seed, with preserving retries and explicit failure statuses. Save SHA-256 manifests, CSV and parquet results, method/cell summaries, paired contrasts, and crossover figures. Compare seed-paired differences rather than differences of independently computed confidence intervals. Record compute profiles and all differences from Phase 3.
